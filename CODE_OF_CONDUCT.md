# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant],
a widely-adopted community standard for open-source projects.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the community. Harassment
of any kind is not tolerated.

The full text is at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/

To report a violation, contact the maintainer at the email in
`SECURITY.md`. All reports are reviewed and investigated.
